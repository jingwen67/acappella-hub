# A Cappella Hub · CUCAC 使用指南 / User Guide

CU Chinese A Cappella 的成员资料、乐谱库和 Solo 投票工具。投票与乐谱库使用同一个账号。  
A hub for CU Chinese A Cappella member profiles, scores, and solo voting. Use the same account for voting and the score library.

**网站入口 / Website：[打开 / Open A Cappella Hub](https://acappella-hub.elenazhang0607.chatgpt.site)**

这是大家实际使用和分享的链接。GitHub 是代码仓库；打开网站不需要下载代码、不需要 GitHub 账号，也不需要运行程序。  
This is the link to use and share. GitHub stores the code; you do not need to download code, have a GitHub account, or run a program to use the website.

## 快速开始 / Quick Start

**推荐大家先把网站添加到手机主屏幕，以后像打开 App 一样进入，查看乐谱和投票更方便。** 无需去 App Store 下载。  
**We recommend adding the website to your phone’s home screen so you can open it like an app to browse scores and vote.** No App Store download is needed.

<a id="mobile"></a>
### 在手机上使用：添加到主屏幕 / On Your Phone: Add to Home Screen

1. 用手机浏览器打开 [A Cappella Hub](https://acappella-hub.elenazhang0607.chatgpt.site)。  
   Open [A Cappella Hub](https://acappella-hub.elenazhang0607.chatgpt.site) in your phone’s browser.
2. **iPhone：** 用 Safari 或 Chrome 打开，在分享菜单中选择 **添加到主屏幕 / Add to Home Screen**，再点击 **添加 / Add**。  
   **iPhone:** Open in Safari or Chrome, choose **Add to Home Screen** from the share menu, then tap **Add**.
3. **其他手机：** 在浏览器菜单中寻找 **添加到主屏幕 / Add to Home Screen** 或安装选项；是否显示及具体名称取决于浏览器。  
   **Other phones:** Look for **Add to Home Screen** or an installation option in the browser menu. Availability and wording depend on the browser.
4. 添加完成后，从手机主屏幕上的 **CUCAC** 图标打开，并登录自己的账号。  
   Open the **CUCAC** icon on your home screen and sign in.

这是网站的主屏幕入口，需要联网使用。已有账号不用重新注册，手机和电脑登录同一个账号即可。  
This is a home screen shortcut to the website and requires an internet connection. If you already have an account, use it on both your phone and computer; no new registration is needed.

### 第一次使用 / Your First Visit

1. 从手机主屏幕的 **CUCAC** 图标进入，或打开上面的网站链接。  
   Open the **CUCAC** home screen icon or the website link above.
2. 第一次使用，点击 **第一次来？注册**，用自己的名字创建账号；已有账号则直接登录。  
   Tap **第一次来？注册** (“First time? Register”) to create an account with your name, or sign in if you already have one.
3. 在 **我的资料 / My profile** 补充个人信息。  
   Fill in your information under **My profile**.
4. 从主页进入 **全体成员 / Members**、**查看/上传乐谱 / Scores** 或 **Solo 投票 / Solo voting**。  
   From the home page, open **Members**, **Scores**, or **Solo voting**.

## 成员日常使用 / Everyday Use

**手机使用推荐：** 先按上面的 [添加到主屏幕步骤](#mobile) 设置好入口。之后直接点击 **CUCAC** 图标，登录后就能查看乐谱、参加 Solo 投票和更新个人资料。  
**Using your phone:** Follow the [home screen setup steps](#mobile) above. Then tap the **CUCAC** icon and sign in to browse scores, vote for solos, and update your profile.

<a id="accounts"></a>
### 1. 注册、登录与账号 / Accounts and Sign-in

- **名字**是登录名：最多 20 个字符，建议使用其他成员能认出的名字。相同名字（不区分英文大小写）不能重复注册。  
  Your **name** is your username, up to 20 characters. Choose a name other members will recognize. Usernames must be unique; capitalization does not distinguish English names.
- **密码**为 4–72 个字符；请使用自己专门为这个网站设置的密码。  
  Your **password** must be 4–72 characters. Use a password dedicated to this website.
- 注册成功会直接进入主页。之后在另一台设备上，使用同一个名字和密码登录即可。  
  Registration takes you directly to the home page. Use the same name and password to sign in on another device.
- 登录名与资料里的 **英文全名 / English full name** 是两个字段：修改全名不会修改登录名。  
  Your username and **English full name** are separate fields. Editing your full name does not change your username.
- 用完共享设备后，点击 **退出 / Log out**。  
  Select **Log out** after using a shared device.
- 忘记密码时，请联系管理员。当前没有邮件找回密码功能；管理员可以重设普通成员的密码。  
  Contact an administrator if you forget your password. There is no email password recovery; administrators can reset regular members’ passwords.

<a id="profile"></a>
### 2. 我的资料 / My Profile

从主页进入 **我的资料**，填写英文全名、代词、声部、学校、毕业年份、专业、Fun fact 和喜欢的食物，再保存。毕业年份填写四位数字，例如 `2027`。  
Open **My profile** from the home page. Fill in your English full name, pronouns, voice part, school, graduation year, major, fun fact, and favorite food, then save. Use a four-digit graduation year, such as `2027`.

可以上传 JPG、PNG、WebP 或 GIF 头像，头像文件需小于 5 MB。保存后的资料会出现在成员名单中，其他登录成员可以查看；只填写你愿意与社团成员分享的信息。  
You can upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF avatar under 5 MB. Your saved profile appears in the member roster and is visible to other signed-in members. Only share information you are comfortable sharing with the group.

<a id="roster"></a>
### 3. 全体成员 / Members

- 成员按 **Active** 和 **Alumni** 分组。  
  Members are grouped into **Active** and **Alumni**.
- 点击成员名字可以查看其资料。  
  Select a member’s name to view their profile.
- 普通成员只能修改自己的资料，不能修改其他人的角色或账号。  
  Regular members can edit only their own profiles, not other members’ roles or accounts.
- Music Director、Arranger 和社团职位标签由管理员或有相应权限的人设置，不能在个人资料里自行获得。  
  Music Director, Arranger, and board position labels are assigned by an administrator or someone with the appropriate permission; you cannot assign them to yourself through your profile.

<a id="voting"></a>
### 4. Solo 投票 / Solo Voting

#### 参加一个轮次 / Join a Round

1. 进入 **Solo 投票**，查看当前轮次的曲目和编曲者。  
   Open **Solo voting** to see the current round’s song and arranger.
2. 想参加 Solo 选拔，点击参选按钮；参选后会出现在候选人列表中。  
   Select the participation button to enter the solo selection. Your name will appear in the candidate list.
3. 如果不再参加，点击退出参选并确认。退出会删除本轮其他人投给你的票。  
   To withdraw, select the withdrawal button and confirm. Withdrawing removes votes cast for you in this round.

没有进行中的轮次时，等待 Music Director 开启。当前同一时间只允许一个轮次进行。  
If no round is active, wait for a Music Director to open one. Only one round can be active at a time.

#### 给候选人投票 / Vote for Candidates

每位候选人有两个选项：  
Each candidate has two options:

| 页面选项 / Option | 含义 / Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **好听！ / Sounds great!** | 觉得 TA 适合唱这首歌的 Solo。 / You think they are a good fit for this song’s solo. |
| **我再听听 / Not sure yet** | 还不确定 TA 是否合适，希望再听听其他人。 / You are not sure yet and would like to hear other candidates. |

- 可以给多位候选人表达意见，并非只能选一个人。  
  You can give feedback on multiple candidates; you are not limited to one.
- 对同一候选人，每个账号最多保留一个选择；点击另一选项会替换原来的选择。  
  Each account can keep one choice per candidate. Selecting the other option replaces your previous choice.
- 再次点击已经选中的选项，会取消这个选择。  
  Selecting an option you have already chosen cancels that choice.
- 选择会自动保存，不需要另外点击“提交”。其他设备登录同一账号，也会读到这些选择。  
  Choices save automatically; no separate submission is needed. Your choices also appear when you sign in with the same account on another device.
- 轮次结束后不能继续修改本轮投票。  
  You cannot change your votes after the round ends.

#### 谁能看结果？ / Who Can See Results?

普通成员可以看到自己选了什么，但不能查看所有人的汇总票数。**Music Director 和该轮指定的 Arranger** 可以查看本轮汇总结果；有资格查看的人也能看到相应历史轮次，最近最多显示 12 个已结束轮次。  
Regular members can see their own choices but not aggregate vote counts. **Music Directors and the round’s assigned Arranger** can view the round’s totals. Eligible viewers can also access the relevant historical rounds; up to the 12 most recent completed rounds are shown.

结果用于帮助 Music Director 和编曲者作决定，系统不会自动指定最终 Solo。管理员身份本身并不自动提供汇总结果查看权限。  
Results help Music Directors and arrangers make their decision; the system does not automatically select the soloist. Being an administrator alone does not grant access to aggregate results.

<a id="scores"></a>
### 5. 查看乐谱 / Browse Scores

1. 从主页进入 **查看/上传乐谱**。  
   Open **Scores** from the home page.
2. 在 **查看乐谱** 区域选择学期，例如 `2026 Fall`。  
   Choose a semester, such as `2026 Fall`, in the score browsing area.
3. 点击曲目文件夹，或学期文件夹链接，在 Google Drive 中查看、下载乐谱。  
   Open a song folder or the semester folder link to view and download scores in Google Drive.

Google 乐谱库需要管理员先配置。若页面提示尚未设置好，联系管理员即可，成员不需要自己配置 Google Cloud。  
An administrator must configure the Google score library first. If the page says setup is incomplete, contact an administrator. Members do not need to configure Google Cloud themselves.

网站账号与 Google 账号不是同一个系统。打开 Drive 文件仍受 Google Drive 的共享权限限制；若提示没有权限，确认登录了正确的 Google 账号，并向社团管理员申请该文件夹的访问权限。  
Your website account and Google account are separate. Drive files still follow Google Drive sharing permissions. If access is denied, check that you are signed in to the correct Google account and ask a club administrator for folder access.

<a id="upload"></a>
### 6. 上传与修改乐谱 / Upload and Edit Scores

只有被指定为 **Arranger** 的账号能上传和修改乐谱；仅拥有 Music Director 或其他社团职位，不会自动获得上传权限。  
Only accounts assigned the **Arranger** role can upload and edit scores. Being a Music Director or holding another club position does not automatically grant upload permission.

#### 上传新曲目 / Upload a New Song

1. 进入 **查看/上传乐谱**，找到 **上传乐谱** 表单。  
   Open **Scores** and find the upload form.
2. 填写曲名。  
   Enter the song title.
3. 选择编曲者，可以多选；名单没有相应名字时，输入名字并点击 **添加**。  
   Select one or more arrangers. If a name is missing, enter it and select **Add**.
4. 选择学期。  
   Choose a semester.
5. 勾选 **大歌 / All members**、**小组 / Small group**，至少选一项，也可以同时选两项。  
   Select **All members**, **Small group**, or both. At least one is required.
6. 选择一个或多个文件，或使用支持文件夹选择的浏览器上传文件夹。  
   Select one or more files, or upload a folder using a browser that supports folder selection.
7. 点击 **上传**，等待成功提示。  
   Select **Upload** and wait for the success message.

文件会放到 `社团大文件夹 → 学期 → 曲名` 下，同时在 Google Sheets 乐谱索引中添加记录。常见乐谱格式包括 PDF、MuseScore `.mscz`、MusicXML `.musicxml` / `.xml` / `.mxl` 和图片。  
Files are stored under `Club root folder → Semester → Song title`, with a record added to the Google Sheets score index. Common formats include PDF, MuseScore `.mscz`, MusicXML `.musicxml` / `.xml` / `.mxl`, and images.

线上版每次上传的**所有文件合计需小于 20 MB**，不是每个文件各有 20 MB。更大的曲目文件夹请分批添加；手机浏览器若不支持选择整个文件夹，可以改选多个文件。  
For the hosted version, **all files in one upload must total less than 20 MB**; the limit is not 20 MB per file. Add larger folders in batches. If your phone’s browser cannot select a whole folder, select multiple files instead.

#### 修改已有曲目 / Edit an Existing Song

1. 点击 **修改已经上传过的曲目**。  
   Select **修改已经上传过的曲目** (“Edit an uploaded song”).
2. 选择原学期，在曲名栏搜索并选中曲目。  
   Choose the original semester, then search for and select the song.
3. 修改曲名、编曲者、性质或目标学期；也可以增加新文件、勾选要删除的旧文件。  
   Edit the title, arrangers, type, or destination semester. You can also add files and select existing files to delete.
4. 点击保存；只有保存后修改才会生效。取消修改会退出编辑。  
   Select **Save** to apply your changes. Canceling exits without saving.

编辑属于共享乐谱库操作，不只修改你自己看到的内容。系统目前允许 Arranger 编辑库中已有曲目，并未限定只能编辑本人上传的曲目；请按照社团约定操作。  
Edits affect the shared library, not just your own view. Arrangers can currently edit existing songs in the library, including songs uploaded by others. Follow the club’s agreed practices.

## 管理员与 Music Director / Administrators and Music Directors

<a id="roles"></a>
### 权限说明 / Roles and Permissions

角色可以组合，例如同一个成员同时为 Music Director 和 Arranger。  
Roles can be combined; a member can be both a Music Director and an Arranger.

| 身份 / Role | 主要权限 / Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| 普通成员 / Regular member | 编辑自己的资料、查看成员和乐谱、参选和投票。 / Edit your own profile, browse members and scores, participate in solo selection, and vote. |
| Arranger | 上传和编辑乐谱；若被指定为某轮的编曲者，可查看该轮结果。 / Upload and edit scores; view results for a round when assigned as its arranger. |
| Music Director | 指定 Arranger、开启和结束投票轮次、查看所有轮次结果、删除轮次。 / Assign Arrangers, open and end rounds, view results for all rounds, and delete rounds. |
| 管理员 / Administrator `admin` | 设置成员角色与 Active/Alumni 状态、管理普通成员账号、配置 Google 乐谱库、结束或删除轮次。 / Set member roles and Active/Alumni status, manage regular member accounts, configure the Google score library, and end or delete rounds. |
| President、Vice President、Secretary、Treasurer、Media Chair | 成员名单中的职位标记，本身不额外授予上传或投票管理权限。 / Board position labels in the roster; these alone do not grant upload or voting management permissions. |

`admin` 是管理账号，不是你自己的成员账号。建议先以自己的名字注册，再用 `admin` 给这个个人账号设置 Music Director / Arranger。管理员密码由负责人保管，不写入此公开 README。  
`admin` is an administrative account, not your personal member account. Register under your own name first, then use `admin` to assign Music Director / Arranger roles to your personal account. The administrator password is kept by the person in charge and is not included in this public README.

<a id="manage-members"></a>
### 1. 指定角色与管理成员 / Assign Roles and Manage Members

1. 用管理员账号登录，进入 **全体成员**。  
   Sign in as an administrator and open **Members**.
2. 点击角色编辑按钮，再点击目标成员名字展开设置。  
   Select the role editing button, then select a member’s name to expand their settings.
3. 勾选 Music Director、Arranger 或其他职位，设置 Active / Alumni。  
   Select Music Director, Arranger, or other positions, and set Active / Alumni status.
4. 点击保存；取消则放弃这次角色编辑。  
   Save your changes, or cancel to discard them.

Music Director 也可进入成员列表指定 Arranger，但不能指定 Music Director 或社团其他管理职位。成员改为 Alumni 时，系统会清除其 Music Director、Arranger 和其他在任职位标签。  
Music Directors can assign Arrangers through the member roster, but cannot assign Music Directors or other board positions. Changing a member to Alumni clears their Music Director, Arranger, and other current position labels.

管理员还可以在展开的成员设置中修改登录名、输入新密码或删除普通成员账号：新密码留空表示保持原密码；重设密码会使该账号原有登录会话失效。删除成员会移除相关参选和投票数据，请先确认对象。此界面不能修改或删除 `admin` 自身。  
Administrators can also change a regular member’s username, enter a new password, or delete the account in the expanded settings. Leave the new password blank to keep the existing password. Resetting a password invalidates existing sign-in sessions. Deleting a member removes related candidacy and voting data, so check the account first. This interface cannot edit or delete `admin` itself.

<a id="rounds"></a>
### 2. 开启、结束与删除轮次 / Manage Voting Rounds

1. Music Director 使用自己的成员账号登录，进入 **Solo 投票**。  
   Sign in with your personal Music Director account and open **Solo voting**.
2. 当前没有进行中的轮次时，填写轮次名称，并选择本轮 Arranger。  
   If no round is active, enter a round name and select its Arranger.
3. Arranger 必须是已获 Arranger 角色的 Active 成员；如果下拉列表为空，先去成员列表指定角色。  
   The Arranger must be an Active member with the Arranger role. If the dropdown is empty, assign the role in the member roster first.
4. 点击开启轮次，让成员参选和投票。  
   Open the round so members can participate and vote.
5. 结束时点击结束轮次并确认。线上版仅允许 Music Director 或管理员执行结束操作；其他成员即使看到按钮，也不能成功结束轮次。  
   Select the end-round button and confirm when finished. The hosted version allows only Music Directors or administrators to end a round, even if another member sees the button.
6. 查看历史结果；需要删除测试轮次时，在轮次管理区选择删除并确认。  
   View historical results. To remove a test round, select delete in the round management area and confirm.

**结束**会保留历史结果；**删除**会移除整个轮次及其参选、投票记录。请用结束来保存正式选拔结果。  
**Ending** a round preserves its results. **Deleting** removes the entire round, its candidates, and votes. End official selection rounds to keep their results.

## 常见问题 / FAQ

| 情况 / Issue | 怎么处理 / What to Do |
| --- | --- |
| 不知道该打开哪个链接 / Which link should I use? | 使用顶部的 **网站入口**。GitHub 是代码仓库，`localhost` 是开发者本机地址。 / Use the **Website** link at the top. GitHub stores the code; `localhost` refers to the developer’s own computer. |
| 注册提示名字已存在 / Username already exists | 尝试用原账号登录；忘记密码则联系管理员，不必重复注册。 / Sign in with your existing account, or contact an administrator if you forgot the password. No new registration is needed. |
| 我是 MD，但看不到上传表单 / I am an MD but cannot upload | 上传还需要 Arranger 角色，且管理员已配置 Google 乐谱库。 / You also need the Arranger role, and the Google score library must be configured. |
| 乐谱库提示尚未设置好 / Score library is not configured | 请管理员完成 Google 连接、设置索引表和大文件夹，并添加学期。 / Ask an administrator to connect Google, set the index sheet and root folder, and add a semester. |
| 没有可以选择的轮次编曲者 / No Arranger is available for a round | 先给至少一位 Active 成员指定 Arranger。 / Assign the Arranger role to at least one Active member first. |

| 没有进行中的投票 / No active round | 等待 MD 开启；已有轮次必须先结束，才能开启下一轮。 / Wait for an MD to open a round. An active round must end before the next can begin. |
